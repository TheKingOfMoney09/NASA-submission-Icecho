# Act III — The listener

**Read aloud. About 42 seconds. 103 words.**

Stop at every full stop. Do not hurry the last three sentences.
Do not perform it. Say it to one person.

---

You have never seen a satellite image.
You have never seen the ice.

You have heard a chord, though.
Six notes, held together.
That was 1979.

The top note goes first, then the next.
Nothing dramatic happens.
That is the point.

Two of the six are gone now.
One year, 2013, two came back.
The chord has not been six since 2007.

What is left is four notes,
a line on a map that never moves,
and an edge that has crossed it.

The ice is about two-thirds of what it was in 1979.
You just heard something most people only ever saw.
